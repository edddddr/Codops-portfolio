Our Story

Founded in Addis Ababa, Habesha Eatery celebrates the rich culinary heritage of Ethiopia. We bring community together around the traditional circular Mesob, sharing hand-rolled Injera layered with slow-simmered Wats and perfectly seasoned Tibs.

Every batch of our signature Berbere spice blend is ground in-house, and our organic Teff is sourced directly from local farmers to deliver an unparalleled, genuine dining experience.
